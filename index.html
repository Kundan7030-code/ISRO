Welcome to India's Space Journey!

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is India's national space agency. It develops satellites, launch vehicles and scientific missions to explore space and benefit people on Earth.

From launching India's first satellite, Aryabhata, to landing Chandrayaan-3 on the Moon, India's space programme shows the power of science, teamwork and determination.

Major Indian Space Missions

Explore some important missions, their destinations and their achievements.

Table 1: Important Space Missions of India
No. Mission Name Picture Launch Year Destination Main Objective Achievement / Special Feature
1 Aryabhata 1975 Earth orbit Conduct scientific experiments. India's first satellite, named after an ancient Indian mathematician.
2 Chandrayaan-1 2008 Moon Study the Moon's surface and minerals. Helped provide evidence of water molecules on the Moon.
3 Mangalyaan
(Mars Orbiter Mission)		 2013 Mars Study the Martian surface and atmosphere. India reached Mars orbit on its first attempt in 2014.
4 Chandrayaan-2 2019 Moon Study lunar topography, minerals and exosphere. Its orbiter continues to support lunar science observations.
5 Chandrayaan-3 2023 Moon Demonstrate a safe landing and rover operations. Successfully landed near the Moon's south polar region on 23 August 2023.
6 Aditya-L1 2023 Sun-Earth L1 region Study the Sun and its outer atmosphere. India's first space-based solar observatory.
7 XPoSat 2024 Earth orbit Study X-ray polarisation from cosmic sources. India's dedicated X-ray polarimetry mission.
8 SpaDeX 2024 Earth orbit Demonstrate docking technology in space. Successfully demonstrated docking in January 2025, an important step for future missions.
